About Upper-Cumberland CleanUp

Upper-Cumberland CleanUp (UC Cleanup) is a community-driven initiative born out of a simple, local need: cleaner, safer streets for everyone. What started as a personal effort to clear litter within my own district in Putnam County evolved into a dedicated platform after a neighbor shared her struggle to walk her dog safely due to trash on her street. By mapping “hot spots” and coordinating efforts, UC Cleanup helps turn collective concern into visible action. While we are currently focused on Putnam County, the project is designed to scale across the entire Upper Cumberland and beyond. I invite you to explore the tool, provide your feedback, or even dive into the source code on GitHub to see how we’re building a more beautiful community together!

How to use this site

  1. Browse the map — no account needed. Existing trash reports appear as markers. Click any marker to see details.
  2. Sign up or log in to participate. Use the buttons in the top-right corner.
  3. Report Trash — select “Report Trash” mode, then click “Place Pin” and tap the map where you spotted litter. Fill in the details and submit.
  4. Mark a Cleanup — switch to “Cleanup Trash” mode, click an existing marker, and hit “Mark Cleaned”. Upload before & after photos if you have them.
  5. View Cleanups — visit the Cleanups page to see completed efforts across the district.
